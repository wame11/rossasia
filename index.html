The Battle of Barnet

Two armies met in thick fog on the high ground by Monken Hadley, and the Wars of the Roses turned on a single morning.

Edward IV, House of York 🌹
Warwick, House of Lancaster 🌹
Before dawnEdward IV marches his army out of London overnight and camps in the dark, closer to Warwick's line than either side realises.
First light, thick fogThe armies are misaligned. Each right wing overlaps the enemy's left, so both flanks are turned at once.
The fatal mistakeOxford's Lancastrians return through the fog and are mistaken for Yorkists. Cries of treason, and Warwick's line collapses.
By mid-morningWarwick the Kingmaker is dead. Edward IV is secure on the throne, and a few weeks later wins again at Tewkesbury.
