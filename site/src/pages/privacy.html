4. Who we share information with

We only share information with the service providers we need to run the business. They act on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare processes enquiry form submissions and stores our client records and uploaded files.
  • GitHub hosts this website and our clients' websites. Like any web host, it processes visitors' IP addresses in server logs for security.
  • Our email provider delivers and stores the emails we exchange with you.
  • Our bank and accountant or HMRC, where needed for payments and tax.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where information is transferred abroad, it is protected by safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK-US Data Bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not go ahead: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client records, invoices and payment records: six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for tax purposes.
  • Website content and files you send us: for as long as we host your website, and up to 12 months afterwards in case you need a copy.
  • Outreach records: up to 24 months after our last contact. Opt-out requests are kept for as long as needed to make sure we respect them.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics that identify you. Our enquiry form may use Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly check that helps to block spam without tracking you across other sites.

  • object to our use of your information, including for direct marketing, at any time;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on consent.
